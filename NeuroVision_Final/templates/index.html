Designed around your research workflow

From an accepted NIfTI file to a transparent prediction report, each stage is presented clearly.

1. Upload NIfTI

Submit a resting-state fMRI scan in .nii or .nii.gz format with the required preprocessing and MNI-space characteristics.

2. Extract connectivity

The system aligns the AAL atlas, extracts 116 ROI signals and computes 6,670 upper-triangle functional-connectivity features.

3. Review prediction

A soft-voting ensemble produces a probability and classification thresholded by the model configuration.

Important limitation

This platform is a research prototype. Its output must not be interpreted as a medical diagnosis or used alone to make treatment decisions.

Results should be reviewed by a qualified healthcare professional alongside clinical history, neurological examination and other appropriate investigations.
